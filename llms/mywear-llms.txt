# VPersonalize

> vPersonalize offers apparel brands and manufacturers an easy way to design and showcase their products, allow their customers to personalize and then instantly generate color-matched, size-graded, production ready patterns for on-demand manufacturing. 

# Company Overview
- Name: vPersonalize Inc.
- Founded: 2014
- Headquarters: Houston, Texas, USA
- Backing: Backed by Accel
- Patents & IP: US 9345280 | US 10706636 | US 9406172 | US 11132836 | US 11970805 | US 9661886


# The platform augments traditional manufacturing workflows with automated pipelines spanning:
- 3D Uniform Builder
- 3D Product Customizer
- Web To Print Automation
- Autonomous Pattern generation
- Automated Size grading
- Print layout automation
- Variable data automation
- Order management
- E-commerce integration
